A few things to know.

Is this website the actual app?

Yes. Open the app and use it without a local service. The current app interface is Chinese, with Chinese and English generation and bilingual product pages.

Why share the video tab audio?

Webpages cannot freely read protected audio or captions from other sites. Consent shares playback, not a downloadable entire audio track. Capture follows playback; pauses, seeking, ads and speed changes affect timestamps. Skipped content is not recovered automatically.

What if I have no GPU?

Use the lightweight Whisper tiny model through CPU / WASM. Speed depends on your device. Pending time is visible, and processing can continue after capture ends. GPU acceleration is optional.

Are ASR and article generation free?

Browser ASR does not call a paid transcription service. Initial use downloads an open model. Article generation uses your selected API; its charges or free tier depend on the provider. Custom services must allow browser CORS; no shared key is bundled.

What happens if recognition fails?

Earlier text and queued audio are retained. Retry on CPU or download pending audio. Partial results are labelled. Editorial correction is not external fact checking; important claims still need verification.
